Expressarte is a 3D printing workbench that runs in your web browser. Split an STL, OBJ or 3MF that is bigger than your printer into bed-sized blocks with labeled pieces and an assembly sheet, design a picture frame or a storage box, make a mold, a lithophane or a relief plaque, separate a multi-part file into one file per part, or fix a broken mesh, with filament, cost and time estimates for every export. There's nothing to install, and your files are processed on your own device and never uploaded.

Giant print: split STL for 3D printing

Scale any STL, OBJ or 3MF to the size you want and cut it into blocks that fit your printer bed, in a large interactive 3D view: explode the parts, watch them slide together, see every part ID floating in 3D, a green or red box showing whether each part fits your bed, and a height chart beside the model for scale: a wooden ruler with a 6 ft man and a 2 ft beagle (or a coin, a soda can, an elephant... for other sizes). The worked example is the 6 ft Giant Robot sample model. Bambu Lab P1S/X1 users can see the exclusion corner and switch to the full build plate; before turning that on, check your filament cutter area so the part stays clear of the cutter. Joints: pegs, dowel pins (with spares), snap clips, dovetail slides, magnet pockets or flat faces for glue. Optional hollowing with vent holes, 50+ printer bed presets, part IDs engraved on a hidden cut face, a PDF assembly sheet, and 3MF build plates or one multi-object 3MF.

Questions

Is Expressarte free?
Are my files uploaded?

No. Expressarte runs entirely in your browser. Models and photos are processed on your own device and never leave it.

Do I need to install anything?
How do I split an STL that is bigger than my 3D printer?

Open the Giant print tool, load your STL, set the finished size and your printer bed size, and Expressarte cuts the model into blocks that fit, with joints so the pieces line up. Download the parts as STL files or 3MF build plates.

Which file formats are supported?

It opens STL, OBJ (any polygon faces, with or without normals) and 3MF models, including multi-object 3MF projects from Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer and OrcaSlicer, plus PNG or JPG images. It saves STL, multi-object 3MF, 3MF build plates, SVG cut sheets and PDF assembly sheets.

Can I separate a multi-part STL into one file per part?

Yes. The Separate parts tool finds every disconnected part, lists its size, volume and estimated filament, lets you untick debris, and saves each part as its own STL (up to 4 free) or all of them as one multi-object 3MF (Pro).

Does it estimate filament and print time?
Can I cut a life-size figure at the arms, legs and neck?
Will the pegs and holes actually fit?

The joint fit check runs on every split: it confirms each peg has a matching hole, the clearance suits the joint type, and there is enough wall around each hole. Anything that needs a look turns red in the 3D view before you export. Dowel pin plates include spare pins.

Safety notice
  • This plate is a cosmetic, decorative cover only. It is not UL-listed, CSA-certified or fire-rated.
  • Do not modify household wiring. Turn power off at the breaker before removing or installing any wall plate.
  • Print in PETG, ASA, or a flame-retardant (UL94 V-0) filament. Avoid PLA near heat-producing dimmers or in hot spots.
  • Keep plastic clear of bare terminals; the plate and its return must not press on wires, test buttons or socket shutters.
  • Printed plates may not meet local electrical codes for permanent installation; check your jurisdiction.
  • Expressarte makes no certification claims. For code-required installs, use listed commercial plates.
Which filament?PETG, ASA, flame-retardant blends
  • PETG: good default toughness and heat resistance.
  • ASA / ABS: better for sun and heat (sunrooms, garages).
  • PETG-V0 / flame-retardant blends: the best choice when you can get them.
  • PLA / PLA+: not recommended for permanent electrical covers.
  • Nylon / PC: advanced; dry it well, warps easily.
